This precision and repeatability is common in software, but new to the 'real world.' I programmed one of these robots to draw spirals. Surprisingly, imperfections abound for such a well-built piece of machinery. The gait of the arm as it moves, the base on which it sits, how it holds and grips the pen—even the humidity of the air and topology of the paper—contribute to chaotic behaviors that reveal subtle patterns of movement in the marks drawn. These singularities are, in a way, the fingerprint of the robot—its characteristic essence of being and moving in response to an imperfect world.
